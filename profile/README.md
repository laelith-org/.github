# About the association "Les bâtisseurs de Laelith" and the laelith.com project

Laelith is a legendary medieval fantasy city born in France in 1985 — a richly detailed and immersive world shaped over nearly four decades by more than 70 authors, illustrators, cartographers, and game designers. Recognized as a symbol of the French Touch in role-playing games, Laelith is appreciated for its unmatched depth, coherence, and longevity. From its complex political, religious, and economic systems to the epic tales and hundreds of locations and characters that inhabit it, Laelith offers a living and vibrant universe. Its meticulous map — featuring more than 20,000 buildings down to the centimeter — testifies to the vision of its creators.

![Laelith Panorama](https://wiki.laelith.com/_media/fr/laelith/laelith.jpg)

To protect and develop this unique creation, the association Les bâtisseurs de Laelith was established in 2026. It brings together many historical authors of The Holy City (versions 1985, 1990, and 2021) as well as new pilgrims eager to participate in the Adventure.

[Laelith.com](https://www.laelith.com) is the community portal used by the association to develop its online activities. Among these first projects already completed, Laelith.com offers:

- A [wiki](https://wiki.laelith.com) detailing with precision the locations, characters, and concepts of Laelith, written and verified by the authors of the 2021 version (already more than 700 references to date),
- An [interactive map](https://map.laelith.com) offering MJ tools and automatic links to the wiki,
- A [lot allocation system](https://www.laelith.com/lot/start) for community members to “bring the city to life” on a daily basis.

Laelith.com is above all an adventure characterized by its community development based on principles of open collaboration and decentralized organization. Here, doers decide! You can all join the architects of Laelith and work on developing the platform in a friendly and passionate atmosphere.

Welcome to Laelith.com — a city in full renaissance, and a world to explore.
